Undergraduate Thesis

A Robust Attention-Based Multimodal Fusion Model with XAI for Thoracic Disease Detection 2026

Team of 5 / Supervisor: Dr. Amitabha Chakrabarty, BRAC University

  • Built a tri-modal diagnostic framework fusing chest X-ray, ECG, and blood laboratory data on the SyntheMIC dataset to predict five thoracic conditions: Atelectasis, Cardiomegaly, Edema, Lung Opacity, and Pleural Effusion.
  • Benchmarked 25 unimodal and fusion models; the best, a decision-level fusion of a CXR encoder, 1D ResNet (ECG), and FT-Transformer (blood labs), reached 0.836 macro-AUC, outperforming the best single modality (CXR, 0.819) and the reproduced XEB-FuseNet baseline (0.755).
  • Extending the baseline with systematic missing-modality robustness evaluation and dual-branch explainability: Grad-CAM for image regions and SHAP for clinical features.
  • Implemented in PyTorch; wrote the thesis and presented a research poster.
